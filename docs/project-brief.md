# PromiseKeeper: Project Brief

## What they do manually today
Operations managers spend hours reviewing historical delivery delays and SLA breaches to manually estimate fulfillment risks for high-value B2B orders.

## Facts, preferences, outcomes, failures, and corrections the agent remembers
* **Facts**: Account SLAs, warehouse configurations.
* **Preferences**: Account-specific delivery routing buffers.
* **Successful outcomes**: Interventions that prevented delays.
* **Failures**: Known bottlenecks (e.g., standard shipping failing for high-value EU orders).
* **Corrections**: Adjusted timeline estimates based on recent real-world deviations.

## Success criteria
The agent must dynamically adjust its predicted delivery timeline based on recalled evidence from past shipments, apply buffers when confidence is low, and block promises that violate contract SLAs.

## Who pays and what cost or repeated work they avoid
B2B Enterprise supply chain teams avoid expensive SLA breach penalties and eliminate manual order-by-order risk assessments.
